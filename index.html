Who we are

Programmers Forge is an independent technology community focused on practical learning, collaboration and real-world problem solving.

✦ Vision

To build a connected network of capable software developers and innovators who collaborate, create useful technology and contribute meaningfully to society.

◆ Mission

To cultivate a community of engaged learners, developers, designers, hackers and innovators through collaborative learning, practical development, peer support and real-world problem-solving.

Core values

The word “Forge” represents a place where ideas are shaped into practical solutions.

Creativity Encourage original thinking, experimentation and new approaches.
Innovation Turn ideas and knowledge into useful products, systems and solutions.
Collaboration Learn from one another and contribute constructively to shared goals.
Practical learning Prioritize building, testing, improving and presenting working projects.
Integrity Respect intellectual property, acknowledge contributions and act honestly.
Growth Remain curious, open to feedback and committed to continuous learning.

Learning tracks

Six-month structured programmes designed to take beginners from fundamentals to portfolio-ready projects.

Web Development

From fundamentals to full-stack. Build real interfaces, backends, databases and deploy working applications.

  • HTML, CSS & JavaScript foundations
  • Frontend & interactive interfaces
  • Backend, databases & APIs
  • Testing, deployment & full-stack projects

Games Development

Learn game logic, design and production. Progress from 2D foundations toward more advanced game systems.

  • Programming foundations & game logic
  • Game design, mechanics & interaction
  • 2D development & core systems
  • Animation, physics, testing & publishing
